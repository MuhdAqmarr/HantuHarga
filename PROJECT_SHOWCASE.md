# Hantu Harga 👻

## Short Description (Punchline)

Scan receipts. Track real grocery prices. Stop overpaying. 👻

## Quick Pitch

Malaysians spend 30-40% of income on groceries — but there's no easy way to know if you're overpaying. The same telur can cost RM2 more just down the road.

HantuHarga uses OpenAI to extract every item and price from scanned receipts. Prices are crowdsourced into a live database so anyone can search, compare, and find the cheapest store in their area.

**Key features:**

- 📸 Snap a receipt → AI extracts items, prices & merchant instantly
- 🔍 Search & compare prices across stores with fuzzy matching
- 🛒 Basket Estimator — pick a grocery list and see which store is cheapest
- 👥 Community basket templates — create & share your own lists

No ads. No corporate data. Just real prices from real receipts.
